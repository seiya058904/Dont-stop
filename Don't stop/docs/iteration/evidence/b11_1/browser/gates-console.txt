## Notes on what these gates do and do not cover

* `C_A_STATIC_FROZEN_LANE_NEVER_TURNED` and `C_A_FROZEN_LANE_FIRED_AT_ITS_FROZEN_GEOMETRY` are the two
  tokens that matter most for this round: they are the browser-level statement that the frozen lane
  which stopped re-clipping still fired at the geometry it had frozen, and that no lane declaring a
  sweep was ever observed as static. Both passed on two independent sessions.
* The dense-laser and dense-laser+root cases are NOT part of this script: they are the stress driver's
  scenarios B/C/D, whose evidence is under `../stress/`.
* `frame_ms_max=150.00` in both perf runs is the same per-round-start hitch the stress census isolates;
  it is present in the BEFORE build too and is not affected by this round's changes.
